StElli Cattery

Who we are

We are a small family cattery breeding Ragdoll cats. We named it after our two little co-breeders — Stella and Ellena. All our cats live with us throughout the house, never in cages, and they are part of the family from day one.

Our goal is to raise fully socialized kittens with a focus on health, appearance and temperament.
