Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal data and what rights you have in connection with it.

We value the trust you place in us when sharing your personal data, and we want you to know exactly how we handle it. This policy describes what data we collect, what we use it for, and what rights you have regarding its processing.

Who is the data controller

The data controller is Petra Vávrová, StElli Ragdoll cattery, Malobratřice, Central Bohemia, Czech Republic, email stellicattery@gmail.com.

What data we process and why

The intake questionnaire and our communication with you
When you write to us through the intake questionnaire, email, or social media, we process the data you provide yourself — typically your name, phone number, email, information about your household and experience with cats, and your preferences regarding a kitten. We use this data solely to contact you, answer your questions, and assess which kitten might be a good fit for your household. The legal basis is your consent and steps taken towards a possible contract.

Automatic language selection
On your first visit, we make an approximate determination of your country based on your IP address, so we can show you the site in the appropriate language. Your IP address is not stored by us — the detected country is saved only locally in your browser, so we don't need to ask again on your next visit.

Cookies
The website does not use any marketing or analytics cookies. The only piece of data stored in your browser is your selected language version (see above) — this is not a tracking technology, and the data never leaves your browser.

Who we share data with

Messages from the forms on this website are delivered to us via Web3Forms, which forwards them to our email — it therefore acts as a processor of your data. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, meaning your browser connects to Google's servers when the page loads. We do not share any other personal data with third parties, and we never sell it or use it for targeted advertising.

How long we keep your data

We keep data from the questionnaire and our communication for as long as needed to handle your inquiry, and for a reasonable period afterwards — usually no more than 2 years from the last contact. If we enter into a contract with you (a reservation or the purchase of a kitten), we keep the related data for as long as required by law, for example for accounting and tax purposes.

Your rights

In connection with the processing of your personal data, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data,
  • have inaccurate data corrected,
  • have your data erased once the reason for processing it no longer applies,
  • restrict processing,
  • object to processing,
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent given before its withdrawal,
  • lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz) if you believe we are processing your data in breach of the law.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at stellicattery@gmail.com.

Data security

We handle your data carefully and protect it with reasonable technical and organisational measures against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

This policy may be updated from time to time, for example in connection with changes in the law or in how the website works. The current version is always available on this page.
