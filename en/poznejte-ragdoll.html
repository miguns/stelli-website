Get to know the Ragdoll

On the temperament, maturing and life with this remarkable breed.

The Ragdoll is one of the larger domestic cat breeds. At first glance it stands out with its semi-long coat, striking blue eyes and distinctive colouring. But its real charm lies above all in its temperament.

It's known as a friendly, sociable and even-tempered breed that often seeks out its person's company and enjoys being part of family life.

A Ragdoll kitten is still, above all, a kitten

Although the Ragdoll is known in adulthood for its calmer nature, a Ragdoll kitten is definitely not a couch potato. It's curious, playful, active and full of energy. It runs, climbs, chases toys and explores the world around it. And that's exactly how it should be.

As it matures, its temperament usually changes gradually and the cat starts showing more of the traits of an adult Ragdoll. Exactly when and how noticeably it settles down is individual, though.

The resulting temperament is shaped not only by genetics but also by the environment the cat grows up in, its socialization, and every experience it gathers along the way. What we give it during the first months is an important foundation, but its personality keeps developing in its new family too, shaped by the environment it lives in, the people and animals it meets, and the experiences it has there.

The Ragdoll matures slowly

Ragdolls are among the slow-maturing breeds. Their body build, coat, expression and overall appearance keep developing over several years. Some traits can therefore only really be judged with the benefit of time.

In breeding, we watch health, type, body build, coat and temperament, and give our cats time to show their true potential.

Life with a Ragdoll

A Ragdoll tends to be very sociable and likes to be wherever something is happening. It may follow you around the house, come to see what you're doing, or simply lie down next to you.

Not every Ragdoll is the same, though. One will be more affectionate, another more independent; one will keep itself busy all day, while another prefers to find a quiet spot near you.

And that, we think, is exactly the charm of this breed. A Ragdoll isn't just a beautiful cat that matches a standard. It's a personality that keeps developing, one you get to know throughout its whole life.
