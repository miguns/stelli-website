How it works

The path to your kitten

1

Contact and getting to know

You write to us via the form, and we discuss your wishes and kitten availability.

2

Reservation

We confirm serious interest in the chosen kitten with a deposit of 40 EUR. After a personal visit and mutual agreement on the reservation, this amount is credited towards the total reservation deposit, whose amount we agree with you in advance.

3

Waiting for the new home

While you wait, we'll keep you updated on how your kitten is doing. You'll get photos, videos and information from veterinary check-ups, as well as details about the neutering procedure and its recovery, if applicable.

Before your kitten arrives, we'll also send you a checklist for preparing your household and tips on making the first days in its new home as easy as possible.

And if the wait feels long, just write to us — we're happy to send more photos or a video. 🐾

4

Leaving for the new home

Your kitten leaves us at 14–16 weeks of age, once it's ready for independent life, properly checked by a vet and equipped for its first days. Before departure, we'll go through all the important information together and arrange the handover.

5

And our care doesn't end there.

Even after your kitten leaves, we remain available for any questions, advice and shared experiences from its new home. We're also always delighted to receive photos and updates on how our former kitten is doing.
