Our work

Education, health tests and socialization — the three pillars our whole breeding programme is built on.

01

Education

In 2025 I completed a cat genetics course at Campus Felinarium — a seven-week programme focused on genetics, health and responsible breeding.

02

Health tests

All our breeding cats are genetically tested for HCM and PKD, plus FeLV and FIV. We also check HCM with a heart ultrasound.

Unfortunately HCM can still appear later even with a negative genetic test result. The PKD genetic test likewise only screens for one common mutation and doesn't rule out other kidney disease — it's worth keeping in mind.

03

Socialization

Kittens grow up in our home with children, used to the everyday routine of a household.

04

Breed standard

We aim to breed to the Ragdoll standard — smaller ears, the right profile, correct eye colour, a balanced temperament and calm nature. The goal is beautiful, typey kittens, first and foremost suitable for further breeding, which is exactly why choosing our breeding cats is so crucial.

Why it matters

Healthy parents, balanced kittens

The investment in genetics and socialization shows in every kitten that leaves us for a new home.

HCM & PKD tests

Genetic tests for HCM and PKD are done for every cat before her first litter, and we also check HCM regularly with a heart ultrasound. Unfortunately, a cat with a pedigree isn't a guarantee of lifelong health — but we do our utmost to make that the case.

FeLV & FIV tests

FeLV (feline leukaemia) and FIV (feline immunodeficiency virus) are serious, incurable viral diseases that spread between cats. We test for them to keep the whole cattery and new owners safe.

Socialization with children

Our kittens grow up amid everyday household bustle — used to sounds, the TV and children around them. This also lets us see which kitten is more sensitive and which is bold and fearless.

Even so, every kitten may behave differently in a new environment — growing up with children doesn't mean it will accept a new home right away. They're still small kittens without their mother or littermates, in unfamiliar surroundings, which is why it's so important to follow our guidance for a smooth adjustment.

Breed standard

We don't want to churn out kittens as pets on an assembly line. We carefully choose our breeding cats for type, health and temperament, so kittens turn out not just beautiful, but suitable for further breeding too.
